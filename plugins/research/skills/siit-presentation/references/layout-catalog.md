# Layout catalogue

Use these as compositional families, not rigid templates. The corresponding SVG previews
under `../assets/previews/` are intentionally generic and safe to reuse.

## 1. Cover

Use for the opening slide. Place a short title in a central-left title block between thin
rules. Keep affiliation, presenter, and date secondary. Do not add a decorative hero image
unless it materially introduces the research problem.

Preview: `assets/previews/01-cover.svg`

## 2. Roadmap or staged research program

Use when several stages form a temporal or conceptual sequence. Put the global framing at
the top, then show a clear stage spine with compact milestones. Highlight the current stage
with blue and the next extension with green.

Preview: `assets/previews/02-roadmap.svg`

## 3. Method pipeline

Use for inputs, representations, modules, and outputs. Give each module a short heading and
one explanatory line. Keep arrows directional and avoid crossing connectors.

Preview: `assets/previews/03-pipeline.svg`

## 4. Metrics and research outputs

Use for a small set of quantitative results plus a compact output summary. Align metric
names, values, targets, and interpretations. Do not imply success solely through color;
include the comparison or criterion in text.

Preview: `assets/previews/04-metrics.svg`

## 5. Recap or conceptual progression

Use near a transition to compress several completed stages into one visual argument. Each
stage should state the learned relation or capability rather than merely repeat a year.

Preview: `assets/previews/05-recap.svg`

## 6. Four-part proposal

Use to explain a research plan, work packages, or four components. Keep the four items
parallel in grammatical and visual structure. Add dependencies only when the sequence is
real; otherwise avoid arrows that falsely imply causality.

Preview: `assets/previews/06-four-part-plan.svg`

## 7. Publications or deliverables

Use for evidence inventories. Give each row a number, type/status, title, short contribution,
and venue or artifact field. Keep descriptions concise enough that the list remains scannable.

Preview: `assets/previews/07-publications.svg`

## Choosing among layouts

- One mechanism with ordered transformations: method pipeline.
- Multiple years or maturity stages: roadmap.
- Several independent parts of one proposal: four-part proposal.
- A few numeric claims requiring interpretation: metrics and research outputs.
- A bridge between completed work and the next research question: recap.
- Evidence supporting credibility: publications or deliverables.

If none fits, preserve the shared title region, typography, palette, spacing, and restrained
component language while designing a content-specific layout.
